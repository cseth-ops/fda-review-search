Nonclinical pharmacology / toxicology

Executive Summary

Inavolisib (ITOVEBI), also known as GDC-0077, RO7113755, and G02967907, is an inhibitor of phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) with inhibitory activity predominantly against PI3Kα, encoded by the PIK3CA gene, and belongs to the pharmacological class of kinase inhibitors. PI3K is a signal transducer downstream of cell surface receptors and regulator of cell metabolism and survival. The PI3K signaling pathway has been reported to be dysregulated in hormone receptor (HR)-positive breast cancer, often due to activating PIK3CA mutations. In this NDA submission, the Applicant submitted nonclinical pharmacology (primary, secondary, safety), ADME/PK, and toxicology (general, developmental, genetic) data to support the approval of inavolisib in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant for the treatment of adults with endocrine-resistant, PIK3CA-mutated, HR-positive, human epidermal growth-factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative, locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer, as detected by an FDA-approved test, following recurrence on or after completing adjuvant endocrine therapy. The proposed recommended dosage and administration of inavolisib is 9 mg taken orally once daily.

In an in vitro biochemical assay, inavolisib inhibited PI3Kα with higher potency compared to the other PI3K isoforms including PI3Kβ, PI3Kδ, and PI3Kγ with inhibitory constant (Ki) values of 0.038 nM, 101.7 nM, 12.8 nM, and 21.8 nM, respectively. The steady-state Cmax for inavolisib is 69 ng/mL (169 nM or ~107 nM unbound) at the recommended dose of 9 mg/day. In a kinase panel of 224 kinases, inavolisib only inhibited 3 kinases (defined as > 80% inhibition at 1 µM) – PI3Kα, PI3Kγ, and PI3KC2β, (96.4%, 96.2%, and 81.7% inhibition at 1 µM, respectively). Inavolisib treatment of a human breast cancer cell line (HCC1954) reported to contain the hotspot H1047R PIK3CA alteration, led to proteasome-dependent depletion of the PI3K catalytic subunit alpha isoform (p110α) protein but not in a human breast cancer cell line (HDQ-P1) reported to be PIK3CA wild type. Similarly, inavolisib treatment resulted in greater increases in apoptosis, as measured by cleaved PARP protein, and decreases in downstream signaling, as measured by pAKTS473, in the PIK3CA-mutated breast cancer cell line (HCC1954) than in the PIK3CA-WT breast cancer cell line (HDQ-P1). Inavolisib treatment of the MCF-7 breast cancer cell line (estrogen receptor [ER]-positive, HER2-negative, and PIK3CAE545K- mutated) resulted in reduced in vitro cell viability alone and a synergistic effect in combination with fulvestrant or palbociclib. Inavolisib showed anti-tumor effects in xenograft mouse models of human breast cancer using KPL-4, HCC1954X1, and MCF7 cell lines. KPL-4 and HCC1954X1 are reported to be ER-negative/HER2-positive and to harbor a hotspot alteration in the p110α catalytic domain (H1047R), resulting in elevated levels of pAKT and constitutive PI3K pathway activation. Reproductive and developmental toxicology

The Applicant’s Position: The embryo-fetal development study in rats identified inavolisib- related dose dependent effects on embryo-fetal development (≥ 0.8 x) that included decreases in fetal body weight and placental weight, post-implantation loss, lower fetal viability, and teratogenicity (fetal external, visceral, and skeletal malformations) (Study 19-0593).

The ICH S9 guidance (ICH 2009) states that studies on (1) fertility and early embryonic development and (2) pre- and postnatal toxicology are not warranted to support marketing authorization applications for pharmaceuticals intended for the treatment of patients with advanced cancer, such as inavolisib. Accordingly, such studies were not performed.

Clinical efficacy

Pivotal Study WO41554 (INAVO120) and Supportive Study GO39374

Trial Design

8.1.1.1. Pivotal Study: WO41554 (INAVO120), Phase III

The Applicant’s Description: Study WO41554 is a Phase III, randomized, double-blind, placebo- controlled, multi-center, global study designed to compare the efficacy, as measured by investigator-assessed PFS, and the safety of the triplet combination of inavolisib with palbociclib and fulvestrant (Inavo+Palbo+Fulv) vs. placebo with palbociclib and fulvestrant (Pbo+Palbo+Fulv) in patients with PIK3CA-mutated, HR+, HER2─ LA/mBC whose disease progressed during treatment or within 12 months of completing adjuvant ET and who had not received prior systemic therapy for locally advanced or metastatic disease.

Patient biomarker eligibility (i.e., positive for a study-eligible PIK3CA mutation) was determined through testing of blood plasma-derived circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) with a next-generation sequencing (NGS) assay performed at the Sponsor-designated central laboratory, or through local testing of ctDNA or tumor tissue with an appropriately-validated NGS or polymerase chain reaction (PCR) assay.

Eligible patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to one of two treatment arms: Inavo+Palbo+Fulv or Pbo+Palbo+Fulv. Inavolisib/placebo was administered at 9 mg PO QD on Days 1-28 of each 28-day cycle; palbociclib was administered at 125 mg PO QD on Days 1-21 of each 28-day cycle; fulvestrant was administered at 500 mg by intramuscular injection on Days 1 and 15 of Cycle 1 and then on Day 1 of each subsequent 28-day cycle, or approximately every 4 weeks. Patients were stratified by: visceral disease (yes or no), endocrine resistance (primary or secondary according to European Society for Medical Oncology Advanced Breast Cancer 4 (ESMO ABC4) guidelines and geographic region (North America/Western Europe, Asia, Other).

Patients received study treatment until unequivocal disease progression as determined by the Investigator, unacceptable toxicity, patient withdrawal of consent, loss to follow-up, death, or study termination.

After randomization, tumor assessments were performed to assess for response every 8 weeks (±7 days) during the first 2 years of study treatment and every 12 weeks (±7 days) thereafter.

Primary efficacy analyses were based on the tumor assessments performed by the local radiologists or Investigators. Response was assessed on the basis of physical examinations,

{Inavolisib} computed tomography (CT) scans, and other imaging modalities as clinically indicated, which could include brain imaging (CT or magnetic resonance imaging [MRI]), MRI scans, and/or bone scan, using Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1 criteria. A retrospective blinded independent central review (BICR) of tumor assessment data was performed to support the primary endpoint of investigator-assessed PFS.

To evaluate the PK properties of inavolisib, palbociclib, and fulvestrant administered in this triplet combination, blood samples were taken at various timepoints before and after dosing.
