Incomplete ossification of the skull bones was also associated with amprenavir treatment. At 750 mg/kg/day, the affected bones included the parietal, interparietal, occipital, squamosal and zygoma.. Conclusion

Since thymic elongation was observed in all rats, no NOAEL could be determined for amprenavir. It is estimated at less than 50 mg/kg. Amprenavir dosing is associated with incomplete ossification of a number of skull bones.
