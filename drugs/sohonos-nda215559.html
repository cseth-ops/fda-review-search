Conduct a prospective observational registry study with safety objectives of comparing palovarotene exposed and unexposed patients with fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (FOP). Evaluate risks of increased flare-up episodes, alterations in growth, and bone fractures. The registry should also collect information on women exposed to palovarotene during pregnancy to assess for adverse events related to pregnancy through the first year postpartum, and birth and developmental outcomes through the infant’s first year of life. Palovarotene pharmacology and RARγ target biology Palovarotene is a retinoic acid receptor (RAR) agonist with selectivity towards the retinoic acid receptor gamma subtype (RARγ), without clinically relevant off-target activity. RAR belongs to the nuclear receptor superfamily, which act as ligand-activated transcription factors, and plays a critical role in cellular proliferation, differentiation, and apoptosis. Published studies describe RARγ as a transcription factor for both tumor oncogenes and tumor suppressor genes, depending on the type of cancer studied (Soprano and Soprano 2002; Brown and Petrie 2021). Upregulation of RARγ was correlated with cancer cell proliferation, metastasis, chemoresistance and poor survival of cancer patients. In fact, there are U.S. listed retinoids approved for the treatment of blood and skin related malignancies.
