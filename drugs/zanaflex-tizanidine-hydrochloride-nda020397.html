Carcinogenesis. Mutagenesis. Impairment of Fertilitvi Two dietary carcinogenicity studies were conducted in rodents. Tizanidine was administered to mice for 78 weeks at doses up to 16mg/kg which is equivalent to 3.2x the maximum recommended human dose on a mg/m~ basis. Tizanidine was also administered to rats for 104 weeks at doses up to 9mg/kg which is equivalent to 3.6x the maximum recommended human dose on a mg/m2 basis. There was an increased incidence of periacinar eosinophilic hepatocytic hypertrophy, but there was no significant increase in incidence of pretumeroua hepatic hyperplastic foci comparing treatment and control groups. There was no statistically significant increase in tumors in either species at these doses. The carcinogenic potential of tizanidine in humans is unknown.
