Review versus label

Where the approved label's nonclinical text does not match what the reviewer reported: a margin stated differently at the same dose, or a tumour finding the review flags that the label does not name. These are leads to check, not errors: labeling can deliberately recompute margins, and some flagged tumours were judged not drug-related elsewhere in the review.
