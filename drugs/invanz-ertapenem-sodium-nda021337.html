INTRODUCTION

L-749,345 (MK-0826) is a carbapenem (beta-lactam) antibiotic that is intended for intravenous and intramuscular use to treat a wide range of bacterial infections.

MK-0826 is an optically active, hygroscopic molecule that contains six chiral centers. It has an

optical rotation of plus 230 degrees. The compound is soluble in water (>500 mg/ml) and has an ultraviolet absorbance maximum at 294 nanometers. ,
