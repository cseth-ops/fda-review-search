2.1. Brief overview of nonclinical findings 5-Azacytidine, a pyrimidine analogue of cytidine, has been studied as an antineoplastic since the 1960s. Reproductive and developmental toxicology

5-Azacytidine caused reproductive and development toxicity in mice and rats. As indicated in the following tables, the embryotoxicity and development toxicity of 5- azacytidine is dose related and specific to the gestational age of the fetus. Of particular note is the increased number of resorptions noted in several studies when treated males were mated with untreated females and the effects on the CNS. The developmental toxicity findings occurred after single and repeat dosing and in the apparent absence of maternal toxicity.
