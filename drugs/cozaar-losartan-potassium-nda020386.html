NDA: 20-386 Date: JUL 2 5 1994 Applicant: Merck Research Laboratories

~: Cozaar (Losartan Potassium Tablets)

Documents Reviewed: Original NDA Submission volumes 1.29 and 1.30 Data on floppy diskette supplied by the sponsor.

I. Backaround: In this NDA submission two animal carcinogenicity studies, one in mice and one in rats, were included. These two studies were intended to assess the carcinogenicity potential of Cozaar tablets in mice and rats when adntinistered orally by gavage once daily at some selected dose levels. The length of the mouse study was 92 w~eks and that of the rat study was 105 weeks. Ms. Katheleen Bongeovanni, HFD-110, requested the Division of Biometrics to perform the statistical review and evaluation of these studies. The results of the review have been discussed with the reviewing pharmacologists Dr. Anthony G. Proakis and the supervisory pharmacologist Dr. Charles A. Resnick.

II.The mouse studv

I Ia. peeign: Two separate experiments, one in male and one in female mice, were conducted. In ~hese two experiments there were three treated groups known as low, medium, and high dose groups, and two control groups known as control 1 and control 2. Two ~undred and fifty male and two hundred and fifty female Crl:CD- l(ICR)BR mice were randomly divided into 5 groups of 50 animals each. T¥e dose levels for the treated male groups were 20, 100, and 300/200 mg{kg/day, and for the female groups were 20, 100, and 500/300/200 mg/kg/day. The animals in the control group receiveci the drug vehicle, deionized water, by gavage once daily.

The animals wer~ checked daily for mortality and morbidity and were examined for the presence of any palpable masses every 4 weeks beginning in drug week 26. A complete histopathological examination was performed on all animals found dead, killed moribund during the experiment or sacrificed at the end of the experiment.

Due to treatment related decreases in body weight gain and increases in mortality, the dosage level for the high dose females was lowered from 500 to 300 mg/kg/day in drug week 28 and form 300 to 200 rng/kg/day in drug week 42. Also due to the same reasons the dosage level for the high dose males was lowered from 300 to 200 mg/kg/day o'l drug week 42.

Survival data analysis: The survival data were analyzed using the extended Mantel-Haenszel test procedure .(Tukey, J. W. , Ciminera, J.L., Heyse, J.F. "Testing the statistical certainty of a response to increasing doses of a drug", Biometrics, Vol. 41, 1985, 295- 301.). This approach compares the entire 'life-table' incidence patterns by summarizing the mortality data at, and cumulating over each week. Testing for trend proceeds sequentially starting with an evaluation through the highest dose of the test compound and stepping downward to the next highest dose each time a statistically significant (Ps.05) trend is observed.
