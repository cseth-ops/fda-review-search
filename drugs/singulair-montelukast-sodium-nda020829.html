Safety pharmacology

Previously submitted ancillary pharmacological studies included effects of montelukast on cardiovascular, renal, gastric, respiratory or CNS and behavioral parameters, where no significant activities were noted at the doses tested. The said studies have been reviewed previously (See attached Pharmacology Reviews by Dr . Choi dated 7/13/92 and by Dr. -Williams dated 02 JUN 97) are more fully discussed in the Overall Summary and Evaluation Section of the Document.
