Three separate’ ‘Studies were done, using 3 separate drug batches (88903, 94702, and 91907). For the study’of the first batch, Salmonella strains TA 1535, TA 97, TA 98, TA 100 and TA 102 were used, and the highest drug concentrations were 30-50 mg/plate. For the other 2 studies, TA 97a was used instead of TA 97, and the highest drug concentration was 5 me/plate. Assays were run with and without metabolic activation (Aroclor-induced rat $9), and with negative (DMSO) and positive controls. Drug did not precipitate at any concentration.
