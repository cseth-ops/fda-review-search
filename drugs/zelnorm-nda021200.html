PHARMACOLOGY :

Predominant symptoms of constipation-prone irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) include abdominal pain and discomfort, and constipation. 5-hydroxytryptamine (5-HT, serotonin) is believed to play a major role in the etiology of IBS. HTF 919 is a partial serotonin type-4 (5-HT,) receptor agonist and can regulate gastrointestinal (GI) motility, intestinal secretion, and possesses an -antinoceptive effect on pain threshold. This would be therapeutically useful in patients with IBS. The pharmacological activities of HTF 919 were characterized in both in vitro and in vivo preparations.
