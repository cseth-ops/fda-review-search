(An Addendum Report) . Date: IND No: 21071 . APR 10 1999 Applicant: SmithKline Beecham Pharmaceuticals Drug Name: Avandia™ (rosiglitazone maleate) Tablets Document Review: Volume 1.5.034 — 1.5.041 Data Source: four diskettes - mice G95501 rat G95500

Pharmacologist: Dr. Herman Rhee (HFD-510) Statistical Reviewer: Moh-Jee Ng, (HFD-715)

. Introduction

In this NDA submission, two animal carcinogenicity studies (one in mice and in rats) were included. A statistical review was conducted by Division of Biometrics II and a statistical review and evaluation report was issued on March 22, 1999.

Dr. Rhee of HFD-510, who is the reviewing pharmacologist of this NDA, has requested Division of Biometrics II to perform a pairwise comparisons on the hemangiosarcoma tumor incidence rates of male mice.

. Results of the Additional Analysis The incidence rates of hemangiosarcoma tumor of male mice are as follow:

There is no significant positive dose-positive relationship in hemangiosarcoma tumor.

A tule for adjusting the effect of multiple testings proposed by Haseman (1983) is used to adjust for the effect of multiple testings in pairwise comparisons. The rule states that in order to keep the overall false-positive rate at the nominal level of approximately 0.1, tumor types with spontaneous tumor rates of 1% or less (rare tumors) should be tested at 0.05 significant level, otherwise (Common tumors) a 0.01 significance level should be used .

This reviewer compared the hemangiosarcoma tumor incidence rates of the combined control groups versus the low dose using the Fisher exact test. Since hemangiosarcoma tumor types with spontaneous tumor rates of 1% or less, it should be tested at 0.05 significant level. The difference is a statistically significant with p=0.012.
