SUMMARY.

This study was conducted to determine the potential neuropharmacological and potential anticonvulsant activity of AL04862-003. The anticonvulsant activity was evaluated only at 30 mg/Kg in a single group of four

fats. The rats were observed for alertness, spontaneous motor activity, ataxia, convulsions, etc. over a 24 hour

period. Electric shock (150 mA for 0.2 seconds) was administered to 4 rats five minutes after iv administration of 30 mg/Kg AL04862 . The control received 0.9% saline.

_ No signs of toxicity were reported over the observation period. The anticonvulsant protection ratio of 2:4 (50%) was established.
