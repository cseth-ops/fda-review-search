Histopathology: In the treated eyes, drug-related changes were limited to minimal to slight subacute inflammation in the vitreous of animals sacrificed on Days 4 and 8. The inflammation was evidenced by neutrophilic and mononuclear cell infiltration in the vitreal areas adjacent to but not including the retina, ciliary body, or iris. The severity of the inflammation was increased from minimal in all animals sacrificed at Day 4 to minimal or slight in each of the three animals sacrificed at Day 8. Inflamimatory cells were not seen in the vitreous of the three animals sacrificed at Day 2.
