Pregnancy outcomes — 2882-1

Safety requirement, 505(o)(3) · approved 2015

A prospective, long-term, observational study in a routine clinical setting of patients aged 3 weeks or older with bile acid synthesis disorders due to single enzyme defects and patients with peroxisomal disorders, including Zellweger spectrum disorders, who exhibit manifestations of liver disease, steatorrhea, fat soluble vitamin deficiency, or a neuropathic process related to a vitamin deficiency.
