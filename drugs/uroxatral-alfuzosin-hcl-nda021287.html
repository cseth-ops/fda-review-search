Summary of individual study findings:

At 1 Hz, Alfuzosin had no effect on any action potential parameter at doses up to | 4M. Action potential duration at both 50% and 90% repolarization were decreased at 30 kM, potentially due to inhibition of the calcium current. The maximum rate of depolarization was reduced (at 10 WM)

“as was the amplitude, slightly, of the action potential (at 30 1M), an effect probably due to

inhibition of the rapid sodium current.

At 0.25 Hz, Alfuzosin had no effect at doses up to 0.1 WM. No arrhythmias, no automatisms of the preparation. and no early afterdepolarizations of the action potential were observed. In addition to lengthening the final repolarizing phase of the action potential, Alfuzosin caused a lowering of the plateau of the action potential at 30 uM.
