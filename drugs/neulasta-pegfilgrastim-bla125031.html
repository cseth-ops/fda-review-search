Key statements

Sentences from the review that state exposure margins, NOAELs or formal conclusions.

  1. The NOAEL ranged from 750 to 1000 pg/kg/ dose via the SC route for these two studies.
    review page 39
  2. Administration of PEG-G-CSF via once weekly SC injections to rats from prior to mating to approximately gestation day 14 (Segment I) revealed a NOAEL of 1000p1g/kg/dose for Fo parental toxicity (reduced body weights) and 1000 j1g/kg/dose for fertility and reproductive function of the Fo animals.
    review page 39
  3. In addition, the SC injection of PEG-G-CSF in pregnant rats, q2d, during gestation (Segment II) resulted ina NOAEL of 1000 pg/kg/day for the dams and 300 p1g/kg/dose for the fetuses.
    review page 39
  4. A NOAEL of 1000 ug/kg/dose for the dams and F)/ F2 offspring was achieved with once weekly SC injections of PEG-G-CSF into pregnant Fo rats in a perinatal/postnatal study.
    review page 40
  5. Pregnant rabbits displayed toxicity to the protein, as SC injections, q2d, during gestation resulted in a NOAEL of only 10 pg/kg/dose.
    review page 40
