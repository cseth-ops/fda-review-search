The results showed a rough dose proportionality with regards to AUC. With the low dose plasma, concentrations were only measurable up to 4-8 hours whereas with the high dose they were >LOQ up to 24 hours, Sponsor suggested that this explains the different half-lives in the 2 dose groups.

Note: It should be pointed out that sponsor has stated that the Plasma samples came from study No. 888197, where as in the original study the 26 week toxicity was registered under study No.884564 and was classified as an interim report.
