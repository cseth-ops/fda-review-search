Review versus label

The label is what prescribers and lawyers read; the review is where the evidence is. This compares the label's pregnancy, carcinogenesis/fertility and animal toxicology text with the reviews for 625 approvals. Of 2042 margins stated in labels, 1016 match a margin in the review; the 244 items below, across 98 approvals, do not, or name a tumour the label leaves out.

These are leads, not errors. Labeling can deliberately recompute a margin, and a tumour flagged by one reviewer (often the statistical review) may have been judged not drug-related by another. Matching is automatic and checked by hand on samples; read both sides.
