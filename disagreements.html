The applicant's position
While TLS can be life threatening/fatal, the impact on the benefit-risk balance of glofitamab is considered low; since the incidence of severe TLS is low and the risk is well characterized, the concept of TLS well understood. Given the overall low rates of TLS as an AE, and prescribers’ general knowledge regarding TLS risk in the aggressive lymphoma population to include management and mitigations strategies, FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s (b) (4) . TLS will be included in Section 6 as a clinically significant adverse reaction occurring in <10% of patients.
